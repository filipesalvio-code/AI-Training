# NexoERP — company, product, and positioning

Base document for marketing and campaign creation.

## Who NexoERP is

NexoERP is a Brazilian cloud financial management software company. The product serves small and medium-sized businesses that have already outgrown financial control done only in spreadsheets, but still do not need a complex corporate ERP.

The system brings together accounts payable, accounts receivable, bank reconciliation, cash flow, payment slips, reports, cost centers, and accounting integrations.

The company is fictional. Numbers, clients, competing brands, and results in these documents are for training and demonstration purposes only.

## Problem we solve

Finance teams lose time copying data between banks, spreadsheets, email, and separate systems. This creates rework, outdated information, and low confidence in the numbers.

NexoERP centralizes the routine and shows what is due, what will be paid, what is still to be received, and how these movements affect cash.

## Main promise

**Financial clarity to decide earlier, not later.**

The promise is not to replace the accountant, eliminate all manual work, or predict the future with certainty. The product organizes information and reduces repetitive tasks to support decision-making.

## Positioning in one sentence

For growing companies that need to control finances without implementing a heavy ERP, NexoERP is the management platform that connects routine, banks, and cash visibility in one place.

## Priority differentiators

1. guided implementation tailored to small teams;
2. simple cash view, with forecast and actual;
3. bank reconciliation integrated into the financial routine;
4. audit trail and approval rules;
5. support in Portuguese with financial context;
6. growth by modules, without requiring the entire suite to be contracted.

## What we sell

We sell organization, visibility, and operational security. We do not sell only a list of features.

A campaign can start with the pain of scattered accounts, but it should end with an observable benefit: a more organized close, less rework, or an early view of commitments.

## What we are not

- a bank or payment institution;
- an accounting firm;
- a system exclusively for large corporations;
- a personal finance app;
- a free tool for issuing payment slips;
- a tax solution capable of serving all municipalities without configuration.
