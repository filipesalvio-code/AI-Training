# Campaign and Channel Briefings

## Campaign 1 — From Spreadsheet to Process

**Objective:** generate qualified opportunities among companies that grew using spreadsheets.

**Audience:** financial manager, administrative coordinator, and business owner at companies with 10 to 150 employees.

**Problem:** control depends on specific files and people; closing and reconciliation take time.

**Message:** the spreadsheet wasn’t wrong. The operation changed. NexoERP creates a shared process with history, permissions, and cash visibility.

**Offer:** financial maturity assessment and demo.

**Channels:** search, LinkedIn, accounting partners, and remarketing.

**Main metric:** qualified meetings. Leads and downloads are intermediate metrics.

## Campaign 2 — Closing with Less Rework

**Objective:** reach teams that spend more than three days reconciling the month.

**Audience:** financial manager and analyst.

**Message:** connect settlements, statements, and classifications to find pending items before closing.

**Required proof:** reconciliation and audit demonstration. Do not publish a savings percentage without an approved study.

**Offer:** 30-minute live session on financial closing.

**Channels:** LinkedIn, email, and professional communities with permission.

## Campaign 3 — Cash Visibility to Decide

**Objective:** create demand among owners who only track bank balance.

**Audience:** partners and directors of small and medium-sized businesses.

**Message:** balance shows the present; projected cash flow includes future commitments and incoming payments.

**Offer:** cash flow simulator with fictional data.

**Channels:** video, YouTube, Instagram, and content media.

## Campaign 4 — Connected Accounting

**Objective:** develop accounting partners and referred leads.

**Audience:** accounting firms that serve ICP companies.

**Message:** controlled access, linked categories, and period closing reduce back-and-forth.

**Offer:** webinar for accountants and client collaboration guide.

**Note:** do not claim that NexoERP replaces accounting software or professional obligations.

## Role of Each Channel

### Google Search

Captures existing intent. Organize groups by problem: accounts payable, reconciliation, cash flow, and spreadsheet replacement. The page needs to respond directly to the search.

### LinkedIn

Good for role, seniority, industry, and consideration-stage content. Avoid targeting so narrow that it prevents learning or exposes individuals.

### Instagram and Video

Generate awareness and explain visual situations. Use concrete examples, captions, and branding in the first seconds without turning the video into a corporate presentation.

### Email

Use only contacts with an applicable legal basis and a clear unsubscribe option. Separate educational nurturing from direct commercial communication.

### Partners

Accountants, consultants, and BPOs need materials that clearly define product limits. Never encourage hidden referrals or testimonials without transparency.

## Checklist Before Launch

- audience and exclusions defined;
- offer available and landing page tested;
- conversion events validated;
- copy reviewed according to brand tone;
- claims supported by source;
- image and testimonial consent archived;
- standardized campaign parameters;
- plan to respond to leads;
- review date and owner defined.

## Campaign Convention

Use `year-month_objective_audience_offer_channel`.
