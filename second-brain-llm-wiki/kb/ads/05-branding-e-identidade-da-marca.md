# NexoERP branding and brand identity

## Essence

NexoERP transforms financial complexity into practical clarity.

## Purpose

Help companies make more informed decisions about their own money.

## Personality

- clear, without being simplistic;
- confident, without being authoritarian;
- approachable, without being overly informal;
- precise, without seeming bureaucratic;
- optimistic, without promising unrealistic results.

## Communication archetype

The primary archetype is the **guide**: it knows the path, explains the risks, and helps people move forward. The secondary archetype is the **organizer**: it gives structure to what was scattered.

The brand is not a hero that saves the company. The customer is the one who makes decisions; the product provides visibility and method.

## Core idea

**Clarity for the next step.**

This idea can appear in campaigns, but it does not need to be repeated literally in every headline.

## Institutional signature

**NexoERP. Financial clarity to decide sooner.**

Do not change it to “always decide better,” “never decide wrong,” or versions that imply a guarantee.

## Message pillars

### Visibility

Know what came in, what went out, and what lies ahead.

### Connection

Accounts, banks, billing, and reports share the same information.

### Operational confidence

Permissions, approvals, and history help keep the process under control.

### Evolution

The company starts with the required routine and adds controls as it grows.

## Short brand story

NexoERP was created after its founders observed healthy companies facing poor decisions because financial information arrived too late. The problem was not a lack of data, but rather data scattered across places and difficult to turn into action. The product was built to connect daily operations to a future-oriented view.

This story is institutional and fictional. Do not invent founder names, investment rounds, or dates without consulting the campaign’s corporate brief.

## Name usage

Write **NexoERP**, without a space. At the beginning of a text, it can be “NexoERP” when referring to the company and “the NexoERP” when referring to the product.

Incorrect forms: Nexo ERP, NEXOerp, Nexo Erp, and NexxoERP.

## Brand vocabulary

Prefer: clarity, organize, connect, track, decide, routine, vision, forecast, actuals, team.

Use with caution: automate, intelligence, control, easy, complete, and real-time. These words need context.

Avoid: revolutionary, infallible, magical, dominate, crush competitors, money on autopilot, and zero effort.

## Images and metaphors

Good metaphors: map, connection, horizon, journey, and clear signs.

Avoid piggy bank, raining money, rocket, generic handshakes, and an executive smiling at an obviously fake chart.

Prefer real work environments, small teams collaborating, routine details, and readable interfaces. Diversity should appear naturally, without stereotypes.
