## Non-approved variations

- `Save 90% of guaranteed time` — unsupported claim and absolute guarantee.
- `Fire spreadsheets forever` — aggressive tone and does not respect the customer’s stage.
- `Your competitor is already using AI in finance` — fear-based, unverifiable claim, and AI without clear relevance.
- `Last spots` — can only be used when there is a real, documented limit.
