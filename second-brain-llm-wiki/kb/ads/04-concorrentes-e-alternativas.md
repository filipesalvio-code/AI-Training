# Competitors and perceived alternatives

Internal material. All competing companies mentioned here are fictional.

## The most common competitor: spreadsheets

Spreadsheets are flexible, familiar, and seemingly free. They work well in small operations managed by just a few people.

Perceived limitations: duplicate versions, broken formulas, lack of auditability, manual updates, and difficulty connecting forecasts to bank statements.

Do not attack spreadsheets as if they were incompetence. They usually helped the company reach its current stage. The right message is: the operation has grown and now needs a shared process.

## ContaClara

Fictional entry-level solution aimed at microbusinesses.

**Perceived strengths:** low price, simple setup, and a good mobile experience.

**Limitations in evaluated accounts:** few approval rules, basic reports, and a reduced limit of cost centers.

**How we differentiate:** structure for a finance team and operational growth.

Avoid saying ContaClara is not secure or that it serves no larger company. We do not have evidence for absolute claims.

## Orbe Gestão

Fictional broad-suite ERP with inventory, production, tax, HR, and finance.

**Perceived strengths:** extensive functional coverage, strong presence in industry, and many customization possibilities.

**Limitations in evaluated accounts:** long implementation and an interface considered complex by small teams.

**How we differentiate:** financial focus, guided implementation, and a shorter learning curve.

When inventory or manufacturing is the central priority, Orbe may be a better fit. Do not hide this from the lead.

## FinPilot

Fictional financial planning and analytics platform.

**Perceived strengths:** advanced dashboards, scenarios, and management consolidation.

**Limitations in evaluated accounts:** depends on another system to execute accounts payable, collections, and daily reconciliation.

**How we differentiate:** combines operations and cash visibility. For sophisticated FP&A, the solutions can be complementary.

## SaldoSimples

Fictional app for freelancers and microbusinesses.

**Perceived strengths:** free on the starter plan, quick to set up, and owner-focused.

**Limitations in evaluated accounts:** simple permissions, low volume, and little separation between roles.

**How we differentiate:** collaboration, auditability, approvals, and controls for a team.

## Bank and isolated tools

Some leads use online banking as their financial system, one tool for payment slips, another for invoices, and a cash spreadsheet.

The argument should not be that these tools are bad. The cost is in fragmentation: repeating records, reconciling numbers, and figuring out which source is up to date.

## Financial BPO

Outsourcing routine work can be appropriate for companies without an internal team. NexoERP is software and does not execute payments or make decisions for the client.

The system can be used jointly by a company and its BPO. Therefore, BPO can also be a partner.

## Rules for comparative advertising

- compare verifiable capabilities, not reputation;
- do not use third-party logos without authorization;
- do not claim that a competitor is insecure, illegal, or abandoning clients;
- record the source and date of any published comparison;
- prefer comparisons by usage profile;
- submit pages with competitor names for legal review.

## Short answer to “why not choose the cheapest?”

The lowest price can be the best choice when routine is simple. NexoERP tends to make more sense when multiple people are involved in finance and the company needs to connect execution, approvals, and cash visibility.
