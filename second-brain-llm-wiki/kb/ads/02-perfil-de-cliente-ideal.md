# Ideal customer profile — ICP

Working version used by marketing and sales.

## Primary ICP

Brazilian companies in services, light distribution, technology, private education, or B2B commerce with:

- annual revenue between R$ 2 million and R$ 80 million;
- 10 to 150 employees;
- a finance team of 1 to 8 people;
- two or more bank accounts;
- at least 300 financial entries per month;
- current control in spreadsheets or in a system without integrated reconciliation;
- a manager who participates in the purchase decision;
- need for cash flow, approval workflows, or cost center control.

The best buying moment usually happens when the company opens a branch, increases its billing volume, hires its first finance leader, or realizes the spreadsheet depends on a single person.

## Priority segments

### B2B services

Agencies, consulting firms, engineering offices, and outsourcing companies. They generally need to control recurring contracts, collections, taxes, and project-level results.

Useful message: track incoming payments and expenses by client or project.

### Light distribution

Regional distributors with a lean administrative operation. They have a high volume of receivables/payables, several bank accounts, and collection needs.

Useful message: centralize accounts and reconcile transactions with less manual work.

### Schools and non-degree courses

Recurring revenue, seasonal delinquency, and a need for cash visibility. NexoERP does not replace the academic system.

Useful message: organize collections and track overdue items.

### Technology companies

SaaS businesses and software houses. They usually use several tools, cards, digital accounts, and cost centers.

Useful message: bring financial operations together and track forecast vs. actual.

## Secondary ICP

Companies with up to 300 employees can use NexoERP when their financial structure is not very complex. These cases should be qualified by the number of legal entities, currencies, approval rules, and required integrations.

## High-intent signals

- search for `replace financial spreadsheet`;
- asks about data import;
- need for payment approvals;
- frequent mismatch between system balance and bank balance;
- financial closing taking several days;
- use of three or more tools for accounts, boletos, and reports;
- expansion to a second unit.

## Low-fit customers

- sole proprietor (MEI) with no team and fewer than 30 monthly transactions;
- individual looking for household budgeting;
- company that only wants to issue a one-off boleto;
- corporation with international consolidation and dozens of currencies;
- business that requires on-premise installation with no internet access;
- company looking for full financial outsourcing;
- operation that will not participate in implementation and master data review.

## Criteria that must not be used

Do not segment or exclude people based on race, religion, sexual orientation, health condition, or other sensitive characteristics. For B2B campaigns, use professional context, industry, company size, and product-related intent.

## Qualification questions

1. How are accounts payable and receivable managed today?
2. How many people are involved in the financial routine?
3. How many bank accounts does the company use?
4. What is the approximate volume of entries per month?
5. How long does financial closing take?
6. Is there a need for approvals or cost center control?
7. What event led the company to look for a solution now?

## Media note

The revenue range helps define the ICP, but it should not appear as a promise of automatic fit. A lead must be qualified by the real complexity of the operation.
