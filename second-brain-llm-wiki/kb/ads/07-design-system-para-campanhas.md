# Design system for ads and campaigns

A concise guide for media assets. Product components have their own library.

## Main colors

**Nexo Navy** — `#102A43`  
Use: headings, dark backgrounds, and high-emphasis text.

**Nexo Blue** — `#1769E0`  
Use: buttons, links, and action elements.

**Nexo Aqua** — `#16A6A1`  
Use: positive data, connections, and supporting details.

**Cloud** — `#F5F8FC`  
Use: light background.

**Ink** — `#17202A`  
Use: primary text on light backgrounds.

**Slate** — `#5D6B7A`  
Use: secondary text.

**White** — `#FFFFFF`  
Use: surfaces and text on dark backgrounds.

## Functional colors

- success: `#18864B`;
- warning: `#B76A00`;
- error: `#C43838`;
- info: `#1769E0`.

Red and green must not be the only way to differentiate data. Use a label, icon, or additional pattern.

## Typography

Primary font: **Inter**. Safe fallback: Arial, sans-serif.

Allowed weights: 400 for body text, 600 for subtitles, and 700 for headings. Avoid weight 300 in media assets, as it loses legibility on small screens.

Headlines should take up no more than three lines in square assets. Essential text must not depend on the platform caption.

## Logo

Use the official blue file on light backgrounds and the white version on Navy backgrounds. Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter `N`.

Do not tilt, add shadow, change colors, compress, outline, or place the logo inside a shape that looks like part of it.

The standalone symbol may only be used in avatars, favicons, and assets where the full brand appears elsewhere.

## Shapes and graphics

Use connected lines, rectangular modules with 12 px corners, and simple charts. Rounded corners communicate approachability; organized lines communicate connection.

Avoid excessive decorative circles, neon gradients, 3D effects, and cryptocurrency-style aesthetics.

## Buttons in assets

Primary button: Nexo Blue background, white text, 8 px radius.  
Secondary button: white background, Nexo Blue border, Nexo Blue text.

Short labels: `View demo`, `Explore platform`, or `Run assessment`.

Do not draw fake buttons in non-clickable assets, such as printed PDFs.

## Photography

Prefer natural light, plausible Brazilian settings, and teams of two to six people. Show collaboration and concrete situations, not people pointing at floating numbers.

When showing an interface, use fictional and legible data. Never use a real customer screenshot without authorization.

## Illustration

Simple geometric style, front view or light isometric view. Strokes with consistent thickness. Blue is dominant; Aqua highlights connection or progress.

## Accessibility

- minimum contrast of 4.5:1 for normal text;
- body text equivalent to at least 16 px on landing pages;
- captions on all videos;
- alt text for informative images;
- do not place important information only inside the image;
- avoid fast or flashing animations.

## Delivery formats

- square feed: 1080 × 1080 px;
- vertical feed: 1080 × 1350 px;
- stories and reels: 1080 × 1920 px;
- horizontal display: 1200 × 628 px;
- LinkedIn document: 1080 × 1350 px per page.

Keep text and logo at least 80 px from the edges in stories and reels. Platform controls may cover the top and bottom areas.
