# Tone of voice and writing guide

## General principle

We write like someone experienced in finance who respects the reader’s time. We explain what’s necessary, provide a clear next step, and don’t use fear to sell.

## Tone characteristics

### Direct

Start with the benefit or the action. Cut generic introductions.

Prefer: `See the bills due this week.`

Avoid: `In an increasingly dynamic world, it is very important to keep track of your company's obligations.`

### Human

Use “you” and “your company.” Acknowledge that changing processes takes work.

Prefer: `Import your data with support from our team.`

Avoid: `Effortless migration in one click.`

### Precise

Replace vague benefits with observable outcomes.

Prefer: `Compare the projected cash flow with the actual amounts.`

Avoid: `Have a much smarter management.`

### Calm

Don’t dramatize delays or disorganization.

Prefer: `If the numbers are scattered, start by gathering accounts and banks.`

Avoid: `Your spreadsheet is destroying the future of the company.`

## Level of formality

Brazilian Portuguese, professional and accessible. Common contractions are accepted on social media, but don’t use fleeting slang. Emojis are optional and limited to one per post. Don’t use emojis in search ads, security communications, or error messages.

## Ad structure

A recommended structure:

1. recognizable situation;
2. practical consequence;
3. how NexoERP helps;
4. call to action.

Example:

> Accounts in the bank, charges in email, and projections in another spreadsheet? Bring your financial routine together in NexoERP and track planned vs. actual in the same flow. Get to know the platform.

## Calls to action

Use according to the stage:

- Get to know NexoERP.
- See how it works.
- Do the financial diagnosis.
- Watch the demo.
- Talk to a specialist.
- Request a proposal.

Avoid `Buy now` in consultative sales, and `Learn more` when there is a more specific action.

## Writing rules

- titles in sentence case, not in all caps;
- short sentences whenever possible;
- explain acronyms on first mention;
- use `accounts payable`, without accent;
- write out numbers from one to nine in running text, except for metrics;
- use `R$ 1,500`, with a space after R$;
- don’t use multiple exclamation marks;
- don’t describe any feature as artificial intelligence if it doesn’t use AI.

## Before and after

Before: `REVOLUTIONIZE YOUR BUSINESS MANAGEMENT NOW!!!`

After: `Organize your finances and see what’s ahead.`

Before: `Total automation so you never have to worry again.`

After: `Reduce repetitive tasks and focus your team on exceptions.`

Before: `The perfect ERP for all companies.`

After: `Financial management for growing small and medium-sized enterprises.`

## Replying to comments

Reply to questions publicly when there is no personal data involved. For complaints, acknowledge the problem and direct the person to a private channel with the ticket identifier. Never ask for full CNPJ, phone number, email, password, or banking data in a public comment.
