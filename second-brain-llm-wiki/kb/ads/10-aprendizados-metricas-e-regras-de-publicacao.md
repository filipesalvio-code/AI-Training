# Learnings, metrics, and publishing rules

Fictional internal notes compiled by the marketing team.

## Messaging learnings

`Fewer spreadsheets` grabs attention, but works better when the copy acknowledges that spreadsheets were useful at first. Messages that mock the current method receive negative comments and less receptive leads.

`Total control` got many clicks and little qualified conversion. In interviews, the phrase sounded generic and authoritarian. `Visibility into planned vs. actual` generated fewer clicks, but more specific conversations.

Reconciliation ads attract analysts and coordinators. Cash flow forecasting ads attract more owners. The landing page and form should reflect this difference.

Creatives with a readable interface outperformed generic stock images in consideration tests. Do not conclude that photography never works; the sample was limited to two campaigns.

## Previous experiments

### Test A — search headline

Variation A: `Financial ERP for small businesses`  
Variation B: `Organize payables, banks, and cash flow`

Variation B generated more demo requests in the observed period. Hypothesis: it describes familiar tasks and reduces ambiguity around the word ERP.

### Test B — call to action

Variation A: `Learn more`  
Variation B: `View demo`

B had a lower click-through rate and a higher share of completed meetings. The team kept `View demo` in decision-stage campaigns.

### Test C — form

The short form increased leads, but removed information used for qualification. The current solution asks for name, work email, company, role, and employee range. Revenue is not required at first contact.

## Metrics by stage

**Awareness:** audience reach, qualified video views, and new visits.

**Consideration:** page engagement, content consumption, and form starts.

**Demand:** conversion, cost per qualified lead, and meeting scheduled.

**Revenue:** accepted opportunities, pipeline value, and customers won.

Do not optimize B2B campaigns only for cheap clicks. Clicks can increase while qualified meetings drop.

## Claims and evidence

Any number about savings, productivity, satisfaction, or return must include:

- source;
- period;
- sample size;
- metric definition;
- approval owner;
- claim expiration date.

Results from one customer cannot be presented as typical results without context. Use `in this case` and describe relevant conditions.

## Testimonials

Testimonials require written authorization for text, name, role, company, logo, image, channels, and usage period.

Small spelling adjustments are allowed. Do not change meaning, combine statements from different people, or write a testimonial for the customer to approve without explicit review.

## Data and privacy

- do not upload a customer list to a media platform without an approved basis and process;
- do not publish screenshots with real names, balances, tax IDs, or bank accounts;
- use fictional data in demos;
- do not request passwords, tokens, or credentials in forms;
- respect unsubscribe requests and communication preferences;
- limit lead access by role.

## Rules for promotions

Discounts must have a period, eligible plans, and documented conditions. Do not artificially raise prices before announcing a discount. Do not use `last chance` if the offer will be renewed under the same conditions.

A free trial may only be advertised when it is actually available for the profile and period stated.

## Advertising incidents

Pause the ad when there is a wrong price, insecure link, unsupported claim, exposed personal data, or unauthorized use of brand and image.

Record a screenshot, campaign, period, and estimated reach. Fix the source, review derivative assets, and notify the responsible teams. Do not delete evidence before internal logging.

## Team’s loose notes

- “ERP” helps with search, but needs to be translated into tasks on social media.
- The owner wants visibility; the analyst wants routine; the manager wants both.
- Showing only a dashboard can make it seem like an analytics tool without operations.
- Security is important, but fear-based ads reduce trust.
- Segment-specific landing pages must change examples, not just swap the title.
- Review mentioned integrations before each campaign, as availability may vary.
