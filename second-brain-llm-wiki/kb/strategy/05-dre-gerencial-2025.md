## Comparison with budget

Gross revenue came in R$ 600 thousand below the R$ 9 million budget. The gap was mainly caused by lower-than-expected sales of the Management plan in the second quarter and delays in the partner program.

The 75% gross margin was two percentage points below target. The main factors were:

- greater use of consulting in implementation;
- infrastructure contracts renewed in U.S. dollars;
- increased ticket volume after a banking migration;
- low automation of support tasks.

Operating expenses were R$ 310 thousand below budget because six hires were postponed. Part of this savings is temporary and does not represent a permanent productivity gain.

## Leadership reading

The company showed it can operate near break-even, but it has not yet reached the growth needed to dilute product and acquisition investments. Cutting implementation indiscriminately would improve margin in the short term and could increase churn later.

The 2026 plan seeks margin gains through faster activation, better packaging, and productivity—not only through team reductions.

## Points under investigation

- support cost by plan and by segment;
- margin of integrations with high technical demand;
- effect of discounts on churn after 12 months;
- service revenue that should be embedded in implementation;
- annual contracts recognized and collected in different periods.
