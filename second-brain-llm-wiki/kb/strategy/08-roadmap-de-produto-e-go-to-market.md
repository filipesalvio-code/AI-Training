# Product roadmap and go-to-market

Problem-oriented roadmap. Dates represent planning intent, not a contractual commitment.

## First quarter — activation foundation

### Product

- setup assistant by segment;
- revised import of people and entries;
- bank account diagnostics before the first OFX;
- internal implementation progress dashboard;
- review of activation telemetry events.

### Go-to-market

- redefine ideal customer criteria in the CRM;
- train sales on real implementation effort;
- launch “from spreadsheet to process” content;
- select the first 15 pilot partners.

Exit criterion: at least 70% of new pilot customers activated within 30 days.

## Second quarter — financial close

### Product

- unified list of reconciliation pending items;
- close and accounting period alerts;
- bulk editing improvements;
- report of items without a category or cost center;
- audit experience update.

### Go-to-market

- campaign on closing with less rework;
- monthly webinar for finance managers;
- segment-specific demo page;
- initial partner certification.

Exit criterion: reduce median time to first reconciliation to 13 days.

## Third quarter — base expansion

### Product

- configuration-based adoption recommendations, with no automatic changes;
- revised approvals and cost centers package;
- accounting connector improvements;
- more granular permissions in the Scale plan;
- reconciliation query API.

### Go-to-market

- in-product campaigns for eligible features;
- Management portfolio health review;
- joint offer with accounting partners;
- accounting connector packaging test.

Exit criterion: five-percentage-point increase in adoption of target features within the cohort.

## Fourth quarter — channel scale

### Product

- partner dashboard with authorized companies;
- standardized permissions for BPO and accountant;
- reusable setup templates;
- capacity improvements for business groups;
- annual security and performance review.

### Go-to-market

- expand the program to 60 certified partners;
- referral campaign with transparent rules;
- planning for 2027 adjustments and packaging;
- virtual year-end closing event.

Exit criterion: 15% of the quarter’s new revenue originated from the channel.

## Discovery items, not yet committed

- AI-assisted categorization;
- probabilistic forecast of receivables delays;
- mobile app for approvals;
- integrations with card acquirers;
- foreign currency consolidation;
- complete customer’s-customer portal.

Discovery does not mean delivery. Each item must demonstrate a frequent problem, eligible audience, manageable risk, and economic impact.

## Dependencies

- reliable product events to measure activation;
- implementation capacity aligned with sales volume;
- renewed bank contracts;
- security review for new partner access;
- updated documentation before each release;
- support trained before mass communication.

## Release rules

1. internal beta with fictional data;
2. pilot with volunteer customers and clear criteria;
3. usage, error, and support monitoring;
4. documentation and rollback plan;
5. communication compatible with real availability;
6. gradual rollout when there is financial risk.

A released feature is not the same as an achieved outcome. Monitoring continues until adoption and expected impact.
