# NexoERP’s strategic thesis for 2026

Internal planning document. NexoERP and all data in this material are fictional.

## Context

NexoERP closed 2025 with 860 active customers, contracted annual recurring revenue of R$ 8.9 million, and an operation close to break-even. The product is recognized for ease of use in finance, but it still depends on assisted implementation and shows low adoption of advanced features.

The market is split among spreadsheets, simple apps, broad ERPs, and specialized tools. NexoERP’s opportunity lies with companies that have outgrown spreadsheets but consider a corporate ERP too complex for their day-to-day operations.

## Three-year ambition

By December 2028, be one of the three most recognized brands in financial management for Brazilian small and midsize companies with structured administrative teams.

Desired outcomes by the end of 2028:

- contracted annual recurring revenue of R$ 35 million;
- 2,400 active customers;
- net revenue retention above 112%;
- EBITDA margin between 15% and 20%;
- at least 30% of new revenue originated by partners;
- standard implementation completed within up to 15 days for 80% of eligible customers.

These numbers are a strategic direction, not a guaranteed forecast.

## Core choice for 2026

The company will not try to become a complete management suite. The focus will be to deepen financial operations and reduce the time between contract signing and the first reliable close in the system.

In one sentence: **grow in the small and midsize business segment by making implementation, reconciliation, and cash visibility a continuous experience.**

## Three priorities

### 1. Predictable activation

The customer needs to register accounts, import entries, connect or import bank data, and complete a first reconciliation. Today, many contracts reach the third month without completing this cycle.

Priority: raise the 30-day activation rate from 64% to 78%.

### 2. Expansion within the customer base

Most customers use accounts payable and receivable, but few adopt approvals, cost center, billing, and accounting integration.

Priority: increase net revenue retention from 103% to 108% by combining lower churn and module adoption.

### 3. Distribution through specialists

Accountants, consultants, and financial BPOs take part in the buying decision, but today there is no consistent partner program.

Priority: have partners originate 15% of new revenue by the fourth quarter.

## What will not be a priority

- payroll;
- advanced inventory management;
- manufacturing;
- international expansion;
- personal finance app;
- permanent free plan;
- exclusive customizations without reuse in the product;
- acquisition of a competitor during 2026.

These decisions may be reviewed if there is a material market change, but they should not enter quarterly planning based only on isolated requests.

## Advantage we seek to build

The sustainable differentiator will not be a single feature. It will be the combination of:

1. a segment-configured financial model;
2. short and measurable implementation;
3. bank data connected to operations;
4. support that understands the user’s context;
5. a partner network capable of guiding processes.

## Decision principles

- improve activation before adding complexity;
- measure real usage, not just feature delivery;
- protect customer trust in financial data;
- automate repetitive tasks while keeping human review for exceptions;
- grow without depending on permanent discounts;
- treat security and privacy as product requirements;
- say no to demands outside the chosen segment.

## Control question

Before approving any relevant initiative, answer: **does this help the ideal customer reach a reliable financial routine faster, or increase the value of that routine?**
