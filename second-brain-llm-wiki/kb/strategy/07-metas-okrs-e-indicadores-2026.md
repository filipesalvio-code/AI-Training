# 2026 Goals, OKRs, and indicators

Objectives guide priorities. Key results are outcome measures, not task lists.

## Objective 1 — Help customers reach a reliable routine faster

Annual key results:

- increase 30-day activation from 64% to 78%;
- reduce median time to first reconciliation from 18 to 10 days;
- increase from 52% to 75% the share of customers who complete their first close in the system by day 45;
- reduce tickets about initial setup by 25%, without reducing help channels;
- achieve implementation satisfaction of at least 4.5 out of 5.

Owners: CCO and VP of Product and Technology.

Activation means registering a financial account, importing or creating entries, importing or connecting a statement, and completing the first reconciliation. Login alone does not count as activation.

## Objective 2 — Grow efficiently in the chosen segment

Annual key results:

- reach R$ 12.6 million in gross revenue;
- end the year with R$ 13.8 million in annual recurring revenue under contract;
- reach 1,100 active customers;
- reduce customer acquisition cost payback period from 14 to 12 months;
- maintain annual gross margin at at least 78%;
- achieve annual EBITDA margin of 10% or more.

Owners: CRO and CFO.

Sales outside the profile will not be accepted just to hit volume if they require structural customization without corresponding pricing.

## Objective 3 — Expand the value delivered to the customer base

Annual key results:

- increase net revenue retention from 103% to 108%;
- reduce average monthly customer churn from 1.1% to 0.9%;
- increase payment approval adoption from 28% to 42% in eligible plans;
- increase cost center adoption from 35% to 50%;
- generate 20% of expansion through recurring add-ons tied to real usage.

Owners: CCO, CRO, and VP of Product and Technology.

## Objective 4 — Build a repeatable partner channel

Annual key results:

- certify 60 partners;
- have 30 partners with at least one qualified referral in the half-year;
- generate 15% of new Q4 revenue through partners;
- keep conversion of partner opportunities equal to or higher than direct sales;
- document joint implementation and account ownership rules.

Owners: CRO and CCO.

## Objective 5 — Operate a reliable platform and a sustainable team

Annual key results:

- monthly availability of at least 99.95%, excluding communicated scheduled maintenance;
- fix critical vulnerabilities within the deadline defined by the security policy;
- keep annual voluntary turnover below 12%;
- fill 40% of open leadership positions through internal movement;
- have 90% of strategic decisions recorded in the decision repository.

Owners: CEO and all executive leaders.

## Guardrail indicators

Growth targets must not degrade:

- rate of product-caused financial incidents;
- post-support satisfaction;
- volume of recurring overtime hours;
- delinquency;
- revenue concentration in a single customer;
- privacy and opt-out complaints;
- average discounts by plan.

## Review cadence

Operational indicators: weekly.  
Key results: monthly.  
Review of goals and assumptions: quarterly.  
Change to annual objective: only with a recorded executive decision.

## Interpretation rules

- a green metric does not offset a hidden critical risk;
- cumulative values must be compared with seasonality;
- a definition change requires preserving the old series;
- the metric owner is not necessarily the person executing all actions;
- goals are ambitious, but do not authorize manipulating dates, discounts, or customer classification.
