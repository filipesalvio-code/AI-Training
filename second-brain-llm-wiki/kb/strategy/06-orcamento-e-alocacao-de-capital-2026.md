## Revenue assumptions

- 1,100 active customers at year-end;
- contracted annual recurring revenue exiting at R$ 13.8 million;
- net revenue retention of 108%;
- 352 new customers and approximately 112 churned customers in the year;
- gradual increase in the share of Management and Scale plans;
- 15% of new revenue originated by partners in the fourth quarter;
- annual adjustment of eligible contracts, with no extraordinary increase in the first half.

## General and Administrative budget — R$ 1.2 million

Includes finance, people, legal, insurance, office, corporate travel, and internal tools.

## Investments outside the operating Income Statement

A reserve of R$ 450 thousand is allocated for equipment, security upgrades, and capitalization of development when applicable. Use requires approval from the CFO and the technical lead.

There is no budget for company acquisitions in 2026.

## Hiring triggers

Open roles are approved by quarter when:

1. cumulative revenue reaches at least 90% of budget;
2. projected cash remains above 12 months of fixed expenses under the conservative scenario;
3. the area demonstrates a need for capacity or capability;
4. the hire does not mask a process problem that should be fixed.

## Approval limits

- up to R$ 10 thousand within budget: area leader;
- from R$ 10 thousand to R$ 50 thousand: leader and CFO;
- above R$ 50 thousand or contract longer than 12 months: CFO and CEO;
- off-budget spending above R$ 100 thousand: Executive Committee and communication to the board.

Recurring amounts are assessed based on the total contract commitment, not only the monthly installment.

## Contingency reserve

The budget keeps R$ 300 thousand unallocated for incidents, exchange-rate variation, and unforeseen obligations. The reserve must not be used to compensate for insufficient campaign or event planning.
