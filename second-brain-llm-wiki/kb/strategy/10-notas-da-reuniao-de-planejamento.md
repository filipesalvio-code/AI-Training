# Notes from the strategic planning meeting

Date: December 18, 2025.  
Participants: Ana, Bruno, Carla, Diego, and Elisa.  
Notes prepared from drafts; final decisions are marked as **Decision**.

## Revenue and segment

Carla argued that the product could grow faster by serving smaller companies through self-service. Diego showed that very small customers churn more, make little use of team controls, and generate a large volume of basic questions.

Elisa noted that a low-cost plan could work if acquisition and support were almost entirely digital, a condition that does not yet exist.

**Decision:** keep the Essential plan, but focus acquisition investment on the Management plan and on the 10 to 150 employees profile.

## Mandatory onboarding

Bruno proposed turning more steps into self-service. Diego agreed with the objective but stated that imports, categories, and opening balance require validation for many customers.

The group distinguished guided onboarding from manual onboarding. The product should provide more guidance; the customer remains responsible for validating their own data.

**Decision:** keep mandatory onboarding in the Management plan during the first half of the year and test a simplified journey in an eligible cohort.

## Margin and customer team

Elisa asked whether a 78% margin would be compatible with increasing the Customer team from 10 to 14 people.

Diego presented the hypothesis that more capacity early on reduces future support tickets and churn. Cohort cost data is still missing to confirm this.

**Decision:** approve two positions in the first quarter; the rest depend on revenue, activation, and volume per consultant.

## Partners

Carla wants 100 registered partners. Ana questioned whether registrations measure value. The group preferred a smaller target of certified and active partners.

A conflict was discussed when a partner refers an account, but the direct team has already started a conversation. Revenue Operations will define an attribution window.

**Decision:** target of 60 certified partners, 30 active in the half-year, and 15% of new Q4 revenue originated by partners.

## Artificial intelligence

There was a suggestion to announce a “financial copilot” as early as Q2. Bruno warned that the team has not validated problem, accuracy, or cost.

Possible applications: suggesting categories, explaining variances, and summarizing pending items. Actions that move money should not happen without confirmation.

**Decision:** keep assisted categorization in discovery. Marketing will not use “copilot” as a product promise in 2026 until there is an approved scope.

## Competition

Ana asked that the company stop reacting to every Orbe Gestão release. Orbe’s broad coverage does not change why customers choose NexoERP for financial depth.

**Decision:** quarterly competitive review. Roadmap changes require evidence of relevant loss in the segment, not just a competitor announcement.

## Budget

The base scenario projects gross revenue of R$ 12.6 million and EBITDA of R$ 1.34 million. The biggest discussion was the timing of hiring.

**Decision:** approve the annual budget with quarterly release of positions. Revenue below 90% of cumulative target pauses new positions, except for security, legal risk, or critical capacity approved by the CEO and CFO.

## Points without a decision

- include billing in the price of the Management plan;
- create a dedicated mobile app for approvals;
- change the entry limit of the Essential plan;
- bring forward the adjustment of old contracts;
- hire a second banking data provider.

These points should return with customer analysis, financial impact, and an owner.

## Actions

- Bruno and Diego: define the official activation event by January 15;
- Carla: present the partner program design by January 31;
- Elisa: publish budget dashboard and hiring triggers by January 20;
- Ana: communicate the three priorities to the company at the year kickoff meeting;
- all leaders: translate annual objectives into first-quarter plans.

## Final note

The plan should be reviewed when facts change, not when execution becomes uncomfortable. Changes need to record the previous assumption, new evidence, and expected impact.
