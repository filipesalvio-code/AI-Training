# Market, competitors, and differentiation

Internal analysis updated in the 2026 planning cycle. Competitors and estimates are fictional.

## Selected market

NexoERP serves the layer between tools for microbusinesses and corporate ERPs. The initial target market includes Brazilian companies with 10 to 150 employees and finance teams of one to eight people.

Internal estimate of the serviceable market:

- 180,000 companies with a compatible operating profile;
- average potential annual spend between R$ 6,000 and R$ 20,000;
- estimated serviceable market between R$ 1.1 billion and R$ 3.6 billion per year.

This estimate combines public data sources, interviews, and internal assumptions. It should not be disclosed as official data without a methodological review.

## Alternative 1 — spreadsheets and database

This is the alternative found in most new opportunities. It has low direct cost, flexibility, and familiarity.

Weak points: dependency on individuals, parallel versions, lack of an audit trail, and manual reconciliation.

Strategy: acknowledge the value of spreadsheets and show when a shared process starts to justify a system.

## ContaClara

Entry-level app for microbusinesses.

**Strength:** fast setup and low price.  
**Perceived weakness:** limited collaboration and approvals.  
**Expected move:** add basic features for teams.  
**NexoERP response:** deepen the Management plan, not compete on free pricing.

## Orbe Gestão

Broad suite for industry and distribution.

**Strength:** wide process coverage and consulting ecosystem.  
**Perceived weakness:** longer implementation for service companies.  
**Expected move:** launch a simplified cloud offering.  
**NexoERP response:** prove time-to-activation and financial specialization.

## FinPilot

Financial planning and analysis tool.

**Strength:** advanced dashboards and scenarios.  
**Perceived weakness:** does not execute the full transactional routine.  
**Expected move:** integrate with ERPs and banks instead of building operations.  
**NexoERP response:** improve native analytics and consider integration, without trying to replicate all of FP&A.

## SaldoSimples

App for freelancers and very small businesses.

**Strength:** digital distribution and free plan.  
**Perceived weakness:** limited support for teams and internal controls.  
**Expected move:** monetize financial services.  
**NexoERP response:** maintain clear segment boundaries and not subsidize software with non-strategic financial products.

## New entrants

Banks and payment platforms may offer basic management integrated with the account. The advantage is native access to transaction flows. The limitation is a partial view when the company uses multiple institutions.

Artificial intelligence tools can reduce classification effort. Without consistent data, however, automation may only accelerate mistakes. NexoERP should adopt AI in scoped tasks, with confirmation and history.

## Differentiation matrix

| Criterion | Spreadsheet | Simple app | Broad ERP | NexoERP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fast initial setup | high | high | low | medium/high |
| Team collaboration | low | medium | high | high |
| Financial depth | variable | low | high | high |
| Inventory and manufacturing | low | low | high | low |
| Reconciliation and cash | manual | medium | high | high |
| Open-ended flexibility | high | low | medium | medium |

This matrix describes patterns, not all products or customers.

## Current gaps at NexoERP

- onboarding still depends heavily on consultants;
- the mobile app covers consultation, not the full operation;
- few native integrations outside major banks;
- advanced reports require export;
- brand is little known outside the Southeast;
- partner program is in an early stage.

## Strategic hypotheses

1. activation speed influences retention more than number of features;
2. partners reduce distrust and implementation cost;
3. bank integration is an entry requirement, not a lasting differentiator;
4. financial context and user experience can differentiate even when features are similar;
5. the segment will accept higher pricing if close becomes more predictable.
