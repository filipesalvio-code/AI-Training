# Products, plans, and revenue sources

Reference table for 2026 planning. Values do not include promotions negotiated before this document.

## Main product

NexoERP Cloud is a subscription-based financial software. All plans include people records, accounts payable, accounts receivable, financial accounts, categories, cash flow, and support through the help center.

## Essential Plan

Reference price: **R$ 399 per month**.

Recommended for companies with a small finance team and simple processes.

Includes:

- up to three users;
- one company;
- up to three financial accounts;
- OFX import;
- basic reports;
- 1,000 entries per month.

Does not include multi-level approvals, API, or advanced cost centers.

## Management Plan

Reference price: **R$ 849 per month**.

This is the main plan for the ideal customer profile.

Includes:

- up to ten users;
- up to three companies;
- integrated bank reconciliation;
- cost centers;
- approval rules;
- billing and bank slips;
- management reports;
- 5,000 entries per month.

## Scale Plan

Reference price: **starting at R$ 1,590 per month**.

Recommended for groups with higher volume, more users, or integration needs.

Includes:

- up to 30 users in the initial package;
- up to ten companies;
- API;
- expanded limits;
- multiple approval levels;
- dedicated success manager;
- specific service level agreement.

Final pricing depends on users, companies, and volume. The plan does not include custom development.

## Add-ons

- additional company: R$ 149 per month;
- package of five users: R$ 99 per month;
- bank billing: starting at R$ 119 per month, plus bank fees;
- advanced accounting connector: R$ 249 per month;
- additional API package: starting at R$ 399 per month;
- extra storage: R$ 79 per month.

## Services

### Guided implementation

Fee between R$ 1,200 and R$ 6,000, depending on the plan, number of companies, and data volume. Includes diagnostics, initial setup, training, and support during the first cycle.

### Assisted migration

Separate quote when data does not fit standard models. The team does not correct accounting or registration data without customer validation.

### Additional training

Remote sessions at R$ 600 per class of up to ten participants. Training included in implementation is not charged again.

## 2025 revenue composition

- subscriptions: 86%;
- implementation and migration: 9%;
- training and other services: 3%;
- usage add-ons: 2%.

The goal is to increase the share of subscriptions and recurring add-ons, without turning services into the main profit center.

## Economics by plan

Essential has simpler acquisition, but higher churn and less room for expansion. Management has the best balance between support cost and revenue. Scale has higher contract value, a longer sales cycle, and greater technical demand.

The Management plan should receive most of the acquisition investment. Essential works as an entry point, and Scale should be sold through a qualified process.

## Pricing principles

- charge according to value and support complexity;
- avoid discounts without a deadline or trade-off;
- preserve a clear option for the main ICP;
- do not hide important limits during the sales process;
- review pricing once per year, without frequent opportunistic changes;
- test packaging before multiplying standalone modules.

## Hypotheses under evaluation

1. including limited automatic reconciliation in Essential may increase activation;
2. bundling billing and the accounting connector in Management may improve retention;
3. requiring mandatory implementation reduces sales in the short term, but improves adoption;
4. pricing per entry is hard to forecast and may discourage usage.

None of these hypotheses is approved as a commercial change.
