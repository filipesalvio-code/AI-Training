# Executive Recommendation

Present a 30-day pilot program with three complementary fronts. The proposal should aim to generate **qualified meetings**, not just increase the volume of leads. The initial focus is on Brazilian growth companies that have already moved beyond spreadsheets but do not yet need a corporate ERP.[^fonte-02]

## Three Ideas

### 1. Intent Capture: “From Spreadsheet to Process”

Create research and LinkedIn campaigns for companies showing clear signs of need: slow closing, discrepancies between bank and system, multiple tools, or expansion to a new unit. The offer is a financial maturity diagnosis followed by a demonstration.[^fonte-02]

- Audience: financial manager, administrative coordinator, and owner.
- Channels: Google Search, LinkedIn, and remarketing.
- Message: “The spreadsheet wasn’t wrong; the operation has changed.”
- CTA: **See demonstration** or **Get financial diagnosis**.
- Indicator: qualified meetings and the ratio of meetings held.

### 2. Demand Generation: Cash Flow Simulator

Publish a simulator with fictitious data that shows the difference between current balance, projected values, and actual values. The material speaks to the owner, who needs to decide on investments, and can be distributed via video, YouTube, Instagram, and content media.[^fonte-03]

- Offer: simulator + invitation for a 30-minute interpretation session.
- Message: “Knowing today’s balance is not enough to decide on the next investment.”
- Nurturing: short sequence with content on cash, receivables, and commitments.
- Indicator: qualified leads, scheduled sessions, and accepted opportunities.

### 3. Trust Channel: Program with Accounting Partners

Invite firms that serve ICP companies to a webinar and a collaboration guide. The proposal should emphasize controlled access, linked categories, and period closing, making it clear that NexoERP does not replace accounting software or professional obligations.[^fonte-09]

- Audience: accountants serving service, light distribution, education, technology, and B2B commerce companies.
- Offer: demonstration webinar and material for use with clients.
- Rule: all referrals and testimonials must be transparent and authorized.
- Indicator: active partners, referred leads, meetings, and opportunities generated by partners.

## How to Present to Management

Structure the meeting in five blocks:

1. **Problem:** cheap leads do not guarantee demand; the target is an operation that shows observable signs of growth and financial disorganization.
2. **Thesis:** combining intent capture, practical education, and trust-based distribution covers discovery, consideration, and decision.
3. **Pilot:** execute one offer per front for 30 days, with landing pages, conversion events, and defined responsibilities.
4. **Measurement:** track conversion, cost per qualified lead, scheduled meetings, held meetings, accepted opportunities, and pipeline. Do not optimize solely for cheap clicks.[^fonte-10]
5. **Decision Requested:** approve the three offers, the capacity to support leads, and the weekly review of the pilot.

## Guardrails

Use the form with name, professional email, company, position, and employee range; revenue is not mandatory on the first contact. Messages with numbers on savings or productivity should only be used with documented evidence, period, sample, and approval. Email and remarketing require legal basis, opt-out, and respect for privacy.[^fonte-10]
