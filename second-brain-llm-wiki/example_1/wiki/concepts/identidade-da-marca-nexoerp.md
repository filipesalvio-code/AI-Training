# Essence and Purpose

NexoERP transforms financial complexity into practical clarity. Its purpose is to help companies make more informed decisions about their own money.[^fonte-05]

## Personality

- clear, without being simplistic;
- confident, without being authoritarian;
- approachable, without being overly informal;
- precise, without seeming bureaucratic;
- optimistic, without promising unrealistic results.

## Archetype and Central Idea

The primary archetype is the **guide**: knows the way, explains the risks, and helps the person move forward. The secondary archetype is the **organizer**: provides structure to what was scattered.

The brand is not a hero that saves the company. The client makes the decisions; the product offers visibility and method. The central idea is **“Clarity for the next step,”** which can appear in campaigns without needing to be repeated in every title.

The institutional tagline is **“NexoERP. Financial clarity to decide sooner.”** Do not replace it with versions that imply guarantees, such as “always decide better” or “never decide wrong.”

## Message Pillars

- **Visibility:** knowing what has come in, what has gone out, and what is ahead.
- **Connection:** accounts, banks, collections, and reports share the same information.
- **Operational Trust:** permissions, approvals, and history help keep the process under control.
- **Evolution:** starting with the necessary routine and adding controls as the company grows.

## Institutional Story

The fictional story of the brand starts with healthy companies facing poor decisions because financial information arrived late. The problem was not the absence of data, but scattered data that was difficult to turn into action. The product was created to link daily routines to future vision.

Do not invent names of founders, investment rounds, or dates without consulting the corporate record of the campaign.

## Use of Name and Vocabulary

Write **NexoERP**, without space. At the beginning of a text, use “the NexoERP” for the company and “the NexoERP” for the product. Avoid “Nexo ERP,” “NEXOerp,” “Nexo Erp,” and “NexxoERP.”

Prefer: clarity, organize, connect, track, decide, routine, vision, forecasted, realized, and team.

Use in context: automate, intelligence, control, easy, complete, and in real-time. Avoid: revolutionary, infallible, magical, dominate, crush competitors, money on autopilot, and zero effort.

## Images and Metaphors

Good metaphors are map, connection, horizon, journey, and clear signals. Avoid piggy banks, money rain, rockets, generic handshakes, and executives smiling at obviously fake graphs.

Prefer real work environments, small teams collaborating, routine details, and readable interfaces. Show diversity naturally and without stereotypes.
