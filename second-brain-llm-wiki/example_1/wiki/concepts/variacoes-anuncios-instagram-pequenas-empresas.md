# Overview

The file [anuncios-instagram-nexoerp.html](../output/2026-08-03-instagram-ads/anuncios-instagram-nexoerp.html)
brings together five visual concepts in HTML/CSS, each with a Story version and a
Post version. The pieces use NexoERP's visual language, editable geometric elements,
and fictional data.[^source-design]

# Variations

1. **The spreadsheet helped. Now the process needs to keep up.** Acknowledges that the
   spreadsheet may have been suitable at first and presents the evolution of the process.
2. **Today's balance doesn't show everything that's ahead.** Works on cash flow for owners and partners.
3. **Closing the month doesn't need to start with a data hunt.** Focuses on reconciliation, pending items, and history for managers and financial analysts.
4. **More people in finance? More clarity for approval.** Presents permissions, responsibilities, and approvals as part of growth.
5. **Accounts, banks, and reports in the same flow.** Speaks to companies that use separate tools and need to connect their routine.[^source-ads]

# Copy Direction

The pieces follow the sequence of recognizable situation, practical consequence,
help from NexoERP, and CTA. The texts avoid fear, absolute promises, false urgency,
and numerical claims without evidence.[^source-tone]

The CTAs were distributed according to intent: diagnosis in discovery,
demonstration in consideration, and contact with a specialist in decision. The priority audience combines professional role, sector, company size, and intent signals, without using sensitive characteristics for segmentation.[^source-icp]
