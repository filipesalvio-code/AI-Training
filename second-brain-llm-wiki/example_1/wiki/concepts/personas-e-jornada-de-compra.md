# Overview

NexoERP's personas are communication archetypes, not real people. They complement the [ideal customer profile](/concepts/ideal-customer-profile.md) by showing who participates in the evaluation, approval, and use of the solution.[^source-03]

## Personas

### Fernanda — Financial Manager

Leads a team of three and is responsible for closing, cash flow, and presenting numbers to the board. She wants to reduce rework, close the month with confidence, create approval rules, and anticipate cash shortages.

Her pain points include scattered data, inconsistent categories, forgotten collections, and reliance on manual spreadsheets. She may oppose due to fear of losing history, lengthy implementation, team resistance, or failure in bank integration. Useful content: migration checklist, cash flow demonstration, reconciliation case, and explanation of permissions. She is the primary user and usually leads the evaluation.

### Marcelo — Owner

Runs a service company with 40 employees and monitors balance, receipts, and commitments without operating the finances daily. He wants to know how much he can invest, reduce surprises, and monitor the company without asking for a new spreadsheet.

His pain points are delayed numbers, overly technical vision, and difficulty separating economic results from bank balance. His main objections are price, team time, and uncertainty about when to adopt an ERP. Useful content: rework cost calculator, executive dashboard, and comparison between spreadsheet and system. He usually approves the budget.

### Caio — Financial Analyst

Handles payments, write-offs, collections, and reconciliation. He wants to reduce typing, find entries quickly, and avoid duplicate collections.

He deals with inconsistent OFX, manual tasks, corrections without history, and urgent requests via message. He may reject a system that seems slow, has few shortcuts, or blocks his work with inadequate permissions. Useful content: short routine video, help center, shortcut list, and demonstration with realistic volume. He influences the decision and can support or block adoption.

### Helena — External Accountant

Serves multiple companies and receives documents at the end of the month. She wants classified information, access to reports, and fewer correction requests.

Her pain points include categories without accounting links, missing documents, and changes after closing. She may oppose excessive access, incompatible exports, or unclear responsibilities between ERP and accounting. Useful content: Accountant profile, period closing, and accounting export. She is an influencer, not necessarily a buyer.

## Buying Journey

| Stage       | Need                                                                 | Recommended Pieces                                                  |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Discovery   | Recognize the problem, even when searching for spreadsheets, cash, or reconciliation instead of ERP. | Diagnosis, educational content, method comparison, and pain-centered ad. |
| Consideration | Compare systems and understand banks, implementation, security, and import. | Demonstration, migration guide, product tour, and segment page.   |
| Decision    | Validate price, effort, integrations, and team involvement.         | Clear proposal, implementation plan, proof of concept, and responses to objections. |
| Adoption    | Register accounts, import history, and adjust the process.          | First 30 days checklist, role-based training, and activation messages. |
| Expansion   | Adopt approvals, cost centers, invoices, and API after the basic routine. | Advanced use cases and maturity diagnosis.                         |
