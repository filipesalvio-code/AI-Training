# Reference Campaigns

## 1. From Spreadsheet to Process

- **Objective:** generate qualified opportunities among companies that have grown using spreadsheets.
- **Audience:** financial manager, administrative coordinator, and business owner with 10 to 150 employees.
- **Problem:** control dependent on files and specific people, with lengthy closing and verification processes.
- **Message:** the spreadsheet wasn't wrong; the operation changed. NexoERP creates a shared process with history, permissions, and cash flow visibility.
- **Offer:** financial maturity diagnosis and demonstration.
- **Channels:** research, LinkedIn, accounting partners, and remarketing.
- **Main Metric:** qualified meetings; leads and downloads are intermediaries.

## 2. Closing with Less Rework

Reaches teams that spend more than three days verifying the month. Talks to financial managers and analysts about connecting entries, statements, and classifications to find pending items before closing. The necessary proof is a reconciliation and audit demonstration; do not publish savings percentages without an approved study. Offer: live session of 30 minutes. Channels: LinkedIn, email, and professional communities with permission.

## 3. Cash Flow for Decision Making

Creates demand among partners and directors who only monitor the bank balance. Explains that the balance shows the present, while the projected cash flow includes future commitments and receipts. Offer: cash flow simulator with fictitious data. Channels: video, YouTube, Instagram, and content media.

## 4. Connected Accounting

Develops accounting partners and referred leads among firms serving companies in the ICP. The message is that controlled access, linked categories, and period closing reduce back-and-forth. Offer: webinar and collaboration guide. Do not claim that NexoERP replaces accounting software or professional obligations.

## Role of Each Channel

| Channel | Role and Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Google Search | Captures existing intent; organize by problem and respond directly to the search on the landing page. |
| LinkedIn | Works on function, seniority, sector, and consideration; avoid overly narrow segmentation. |
| Instagram and Video | Generate recognition and explain visual situations; use concrete examples, captions, and branding in the first few seconds. |
| Email | Use only contacts with legal basis and unsubscribe options; separate educational nurturing from direct sales. |
| Partners | Provide materials with clear product limits; do not encourage hidden referrals or testimonials without transparency. |

## Launch Checklist

- audience and exclusions defined;
- offer available and landing page tested;
- conversion events validated;
- text reviewed according to brand tone;
- claims supported by source;
- image consent and testimonial archived;
- standardized campaign parameters;
- plan to respond to leads;
- review date and responsible person defined.

## Campaign Convention

Use `year-month_objective_audience_offer_channel`.

Example: `2026-08_demo-financial-manager_diagnosis_linkedin`.
