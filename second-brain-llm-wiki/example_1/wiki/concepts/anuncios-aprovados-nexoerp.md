# Usage

The examples below are approved as references but should be adapted to the
targeting, offer, and character limits of each channel.[^source-08] The
copy should follow the [tone of voice guide](/concepts/tone-of-voice-and-copy-guide.md).

## Research

### Replace Spreadsheet

- **Titles:** Financial control without scattered spreadsheets; Meet NexoERP;
  Planned and actual in one flow.
- **Description:** Organize accounts payable, receivables, and banks. See how
  NexoERP supports your finance team's routine.
- **Recommended page:** product overview.

### Bank Reconciliation

- **Titles:** Bank reconciliation for companies; Less manual checking;
  NexoERP demonstration.
- **Description:** Import statements, find entries, and track reconciliation
  pending items in a single routine.
- Do not use “100% automatic reconciliation”.

## Remarketing, Content, and Email

- **Price Remarketing:** invite for a demonstration based on the company's routine, with the title “Clear Your Implementation Doubts” and CTA “Talk to an Expert”. Do not use false urgency or permanent countdowns.
- **Content Ad:** present the difference between cash and profit with the title “Guide: Cash vs. Accrual” and CTA “Download Guide”.
- **Sponsored Email:** subject “12 Points to Review Before Switching Your Financial Spreadsheet”; content about registrations, history, banks, approvals, and team; CTA “Access Checklist”.

## Unapproved Variations

- “Save 90% of guaranteed time”: unverified data and absolute guarantee.
- “Fire spreadsheets forever”: aggressive tone and disrespect to the client's stage.
- “Your competitor is already using AI in finance”: fear, unverifiable claim, and AI without clear relation.
- “Last spots”: only use when there is a real and documented limit.
