# Overview

The campaign design system organizes a clear, connected, and readable visual language for NexoERP media pieces. The internal components of the product have their own library.[^fonte-07]

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nexo Navy | `#102A43` | Titles, dark backgrounds, and highlighted text. |
| Nexo Blue | `#1769E0` | Buttons, links, and action elements. |
| Nexo Aqua | `#16A6A1` | Positive data, connections, and supporting details. |
| Cloud | `#F5F8FC` | Light background. |
| Ink | `#17202A` | Main text on light background. |
| Slate | `#5D6B7A` | Secondary text. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Surfaces and text over dark backgrounds. |

Functional colors: success `#18864B`, attention `#B76A00`, error `#C43838`, and information `#1769E0`. Red and green should not be the only means of differentiating data; add labels, icons, or patterns.

## Typography and layout

The main font is **Inter**, with **Arial, sans-serif** as an alternative. Use weight 400 for body text, 600 for subtitles, and 700 for titles. Avoid 300 in media. In square pieces, titles should occupy a maximum of three lines, and essential text must not rely on platform captions.

## Logo, shapes, and buttons

Use the official blue file on a light background and the white on Navy, maintaining a clear area equivalent to the height of the letter `N`. Do not tilt, shade, change colors, compress, outline, or place the logo in a way that makes it look like part of it. The isolated symbol is reserved for avatars, favicons, and pieces with the full brand at another point.

Use connected lines, rectangular modules with 12 px corners, and simple graphics. Avoid excessive decorative circles, neon gradients, 3D effects, and cryptocurrency aesthetics.

The primary button has a Nexo Blue background, white text, and an 8 px radius. The secondary button has a white background, border, and Nexo Blue text. Short labels include `View demo`, `Explore platform`, and `Run diagnosis`. Do not design a fake button in a non-clickable piece, such as a printed PDF.

## Photography and illustration

Prefer natural light, plausible Brazilian settings, and teams of two to six people in concrete collaboration situations. Interfaces should use fictional and readable data; do not use real customer captures without authorization.

Illustrations should be geometric, frontal, or slightly isometric, with consistent strokes. Blue is dominant, and Aqua highlights connection or progress.

## Accessibility and formats

- minimum contrast of 4.5:1 for normal text;
- body text equivalent to at least 16 px on landing pages;
- captions on all videos;
- alternative text for informative images;
- no important information solely within the image;
- avoid fast or flashing animations.

| Piece | Format |
| --- | --- |
| Square feed | 1080 × 1080 px |
| Vertical feed | 1080 × 1350 px |
| Stories and reels | 1080 × 1920 px |
| Horizontal display | 1200 × 628 px |
| LinkedIn document | 1080 × 1350 px per page |

In stories and reels, keep text and logo at least 80 px from the edges to avoid being covered by platform controls.
