# Overview

For growing companies that need to control finances without implementing a heavy ERP, NexoERP is the management platform that connects routine, banks, and cash visibility in one place.[^source-01]

## Main Promise

**Financial clarity to decide sooner, not later.**

This promise means organizing information and reducing repetitive tasks to support decisions. It does not promise to replace the accountant, eliminate all manual work, or predict the future with certainty.[^source-01]

## Key Differentiators

- guided implementation suitable for small teams;
- simple cash visibility, with forecast and actual;
- bank reconciliation integrated into the financial routine;
- audit trail and approval rules;
- support in Portuguese with financial context;
- growth through modules, without requiring the hiring of the entire suite.[^source-01]

## Communication Rule

Communication should sell organization, visibility, and operational security, not just a list of features. A campaign may start with the pain of scattered accounts but should end with an observable benefit, such as more organized closing, less rework, or advanced visibility of commitments.[^source-01]

### Approved Phrases

- Organized finances for the company to grow.
- See the forecast and actual in the same flow.
- Fewer scattered spreadsheets. More clarity about cash.
- Accounts, banks, and reports connected in one place.
- Financial control that keeps pace with your company.

### Phrases to Avoid

- Never make a mistake with your finances again.
- Guaranteed profit in a few days.
- The only ERP your company needs.
- Replace your financial team.
- 100% automatic reconciliation with any bank.
- Instant implementation with no effort.[^source-01]
