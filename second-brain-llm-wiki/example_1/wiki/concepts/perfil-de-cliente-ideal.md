# Overview

The ideal customer of NexoERP is a growing Brazilian company whose financial routine has already surpassed spreadsheets but can still be managed without the complexity of a corporate ERP.[^source-02] This profile embodies the audience of the [NexoERP positioning](/concepts/nexoerp-positioning.md).

## Main ICP

The best fit usually includes:

- service, light distribution, technology, private education, or B2B commerce companies;
- annual revenue between R$ 2 million and R$ 80 million;
- 10 to 150 employees and a finance team of 1 to 8 people;
- two or more bank accounts and at least 300 financial transactions per month;
- control in spreadsheets or a system without integrated reconciliation;
- a manager involved in the purchasing decision;
- need for cash flow, approval, or cost center.[^source-02]

## Priority Segments

| Segment | Need or useful message |
| --- | --- |
| B2B Services | Track receipts and expenses by customer or project. |
| Light Distribution | Centralize accounts and reconcile transactions with less manual work. |
| Schools and Free Courses | Organize collections and track overdue titles without replacing the academic system. |
| Technology Companies | Consolidate financial operations and track forecast versus actual. |

Companies with up to 300 employees can form the secondary ICP when the financial structure is not very complex. In these cases, the number of companies, currencies, approval rules, and necessary integrations should be evaluated.

## Purchase Moment and Intent

The purchase moment usually arises when the company opens a branch, increases the volume of collections, hires its first financial leader, or realizes that the spreadsheet relies on a single person.[^source-02]

High intent signals:

- seeking to replace the financial spreadsheet;
- inquiries about data import;
- need for payment approval;
- frequent discrepancies between system and bank balances;
- financial closing that takes several days;
- use of three or more tools for accounts, invoices, and reports;
- expansion to a second unit.[^source-02]

## Low Fit

The following should be treated as low fit: MEI without a team and with fewer than 30 monthly transactions; individuals seeking personal budgeting; companies wanting only a single invoice; corporations with international consolidation and dozens of currencies; businesses requiring local installation; companies looking for complete outsourcing of finance; and operations that do not accept participation in implementation and record review.[^source-02]

## Qualification

Recommended questions:

1. How are accounts payable and receivable managed today?
2. How many people are involved in the financial routine?
3. How many bank accounts does the company operate?
4. What is the approximate volume of transactions per month?
5. How long does the financial closing take?
6. Is there a need for approval or cost center?
7. What event prompted the company to seek a solution now?

Revenue helps define the ICP but does not guarantee automatic suitability. Qualification should consider the actual complexity of the operation.[^source-02]

## Ethical Segmentation Criteria

B2B campaigns should use professional context, industry, company size, and product-related intent. People should not be segmented or excluded based on race, religion, sexual orientation, health condition, or other sensitive characteristics.[^source-02]
