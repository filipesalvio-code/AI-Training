# Overview

This template serves for NexoERP ads directed at small and medium-sized growing businesses whose financial routine has outgrown spreadsheets but does not yet require an enterprise ERP.[^source-icp] The structure should sell organization, visibility, and operational security, not just list features.[^source-positioning]

# Structure

## 1. Recognizable Situation

Start with a scene that the audience identifies in their routine. Use a question or a short phrase, without dramatizing:

> Bank accounts, invoices in the email, and projections in another spreadsheet?

Adapt the situation to the audience: slow closing for the financial manager, a balance with no future vision for the owner, or repetitive data entry for the financial analyst.[^source-ads]

## 2. Practical Consequence

Show the observable effect of the problem, such as rework, divergent data, lengthy reconciliations, or difficulty anticipating commitments. Avoid using fear, guilt, or absolute promises.[^source-tone]

> When each piece of information is in a different place, closing the month and deciding on the next investment becomes more difficult.

## 3. How the Solution Helps

Connect the pain to a specific benefit of NexoERP. Prioritize one message per ad:

- gather accounts, banks, and reports in one place;
- compare projected cash with actual cash;
- track reconciliation, approvals, and history;
- organize the financial routine with guided implementation.

Avoid claiming that the solution replaces the team, eliminates all manual work, or reconciles automatically with any bank.[^source-positioning]

## 4. Next Action

Choose a CTA compatible with the stage of the journey:

- discovery: **Get the financial diagnosis**;
- consideration: **See how it works** or **Watch the demo**;
- decision: **Request a proposal** or **Talk to a specialist**.

In a consultative sale, prefer a specific action over “Learn more” or “Buy now.”[^source-tone]

# Ready Example

**Title:** Organize your finances and see what’s ahead

**Text:** Has your company grown and accounts, banks, and projections become scattered? Gather your financial routine in NexoERP, compare the projected with the actual, and track commitments in one flow.

**CTA:** Watch the demo.

**Audience:** owners and financial managers of companies with ten to 150 employees, especially B2B service businesses, light distribution, private education, and technology.[^source-icp]

**Offer:** demo guided by the company’s routine.

# Channel Adaptation

| Channel | Recommended Structure | Suggested CTA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search | title with the problem + direct description of the solution | See how it works |
| LinkedIn | function pain + benefit + growth context | Request demo |
| Instagram and video | visual scene of disorganization + transformation + brand | Get the financial diagnosis |
| Email | subject with checklist or specific problem + educational content | Access checklist |

The ad should lead to a landing page consistent with the promise and the offer. Before publication, validate audience, exclusions, conversion, claims, consents, and lead support plan.[^source-briefing]

# Review Checklist

- Does the first sentence describe a real situation of the audience?
- Is the benefit observable and not a vague promise?
- Is there only one main message?
- Does the CTA correspond to the stage of the journey?
- Does every numerical claim have a source, period, and approval?
- Does the piece avoid fear, false urgency, and sensitive characteristic segmentation?
- Do the ad and landing page use the same offer?
