# Messaging Learnings

“Fewer spreadsheets” grabs attention, but it works better when acknowledging that the spreadsheet was useful at the beginning. “Total control” generated many clicks but little qualified conversion; “view of forecast and actual” generated fewer clicks and more specific conversations. Reconciliation ads attract analysts and coordinators, while cash projection ads attract owners.[^source-10]

Creatives with a readable interface outperformed generic stock images in consideration tests, but the sample was limited to two campaigns: do not conclude that photography never works.

## Experiments

| Test | Variations | Observation and Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — search title | “Financial ERP for small businesses” vs. “Organize accounts, banks, and cash flow” | The second generated more demo requests; it may reduce the ambiguity of “ERP.” |
| B — CTA | “Learn more” vs. “See demo” | The second had fewer clicks but a higher proportion of meetings held; keep it in decision. |
| C — form | Short vs. name, professional email, company, position, and employee range | The short version increased leads but lost qualification data; maintain the current fields. Revenue is not mandatory on the first contact. |

## Metrics by Stage

- **Awareness:** reach in the audience, qualified video views, and new visits.
- **Consideration:** engagement with the page, content consumption, and form initiation.
- **Demand:** conversion, cost per qualified lead, and scheduled meeting.
- **Revenue:** accepted opportunities, pipeline value, and acquired customers.

Do not optimize B2B campaigns solely for cheap clicks: clicks may grow while qualified meetings decline.

## Claims and Evidence

Any number related to savings, productivity, satisfaction, or return needs a source, period, sample size, metric definition, approval authority, and expiration date. A customer's result is not a typical result without context; use “in this case” and describe relevant conditions.

## Testimonials and Data

Testimonials require written authorization for text, name, position, company, logo, image, channels, and period. Minor spelling adjustments are allowed, but the meaning should not change, statements should not be combined, or testimonials written for approval without explicit review.

Do not upload a client list in media without an approved basis and process; do not publish real names, balances, CNPJ, or bank account numbers; use fictitious data in demonstrations; do not request passwords, tokens, or credentials; respect opt-out requests and limit access to leads according to role.

## Promotions

Discounts must inform the period, eligible plans, and documented conditions. Do not artificially raise prices before announcing a discount. Do not use “last chance” if the offer will be renewed under the same conditions. A free trial can only be advertised when available for the profile and for the informed period.

## Advertising Incidents

Pause the ad when there is an incorrect price, insecure link, unsubstantiated claim, exposed personal data, or unauthorized use of brand or image. Record capture, campaign, period, and estimated reach; correct the source, review derived pieces, and notify the responsible areas. Do not delete evidence before internal registration.
