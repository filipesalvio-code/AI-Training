# General Principle

We write as an experienced finance professional who respects the reader's time: we explain what is necessary, provide a clear next step, and do not use fear to sell.[^fonte-06]

## Tone Characteristics

- **Direct:** start with the benefit or action and cut generic introductions. Prefer “See the bills due this week.”
- **Human:** use “you” and “your company” and acknowledge that changing processes requires work. Prefer “Import your data with our team's support.”
- **Precise:** replace vague benefits with observable results. Prefer “Compare the projected cash flow with the actual amounts.”
- **Calm:** do not dramatize delays or disorganization. Prefer “If the numbers are scattered, start by gathering accounts and banks.”

Avoid, respectively, abstract introductions, promises of effortless migration, expressions like “much smarter management,” and dramatizations like “your spreadsheet is destroying the company's future.”

## Formality

Use professional and accessible Brazilian Portuguese. Common contractions are acceptable on social media, but avoid transient slang. Emojis are optional and limited to one per post; do not use emojis in search ads, security communications, or error messages.

## Ad Structure

1. recognizable situation;
2. practical consequence;
3. how NexoERP helps;
4. call to action.

Example: “Bank accounts, invoices in your email, and projections in another spreadsheet? Gather your financial routine in NexoERP and track projected and actual in the same flow. Discover the platform.”

## Calls to Action

- Discover NexoERP.
- See how it works.
- Take the financial diagnosis.
- Watch the demonstration.
- Talk to a specialist.
- Request a proposal.

Use the CTA according to the stage. Avoid “Buy now” in consultative sales and “Learn more” when there is a more specific action.

## Writing Rules

- use normal case for titles;
- prefer short sentences;
- explain acronyms on first occurrence;
- write “contas a pagar,” without accent;
- write numbers one to nine in full in running text, except for metrics;
- use `R$ 1,500`, with a space after `R$`;
- do not use multiple exclamation points;
- do not refer to an artificial intelligence feature if it does not use AI.

Examples of direction: “Organize your finances and see what’s ahead” is preferable to “REVOLUTIONIZE YOUR BUSINESS MANAGEMENT NOW!!!”; “Reduce repetitive tasks and focus your team on exceptions” is preferable to “Total automation so you never have to worry again.”

## Public Responses

Respond to inquiries without exposing personal data. In complaints, acknowledge the problem and direct to a private channel with the ticket identifier. Never ask for complete CNPJ, phone number, email, password, or banking data in public comments.
