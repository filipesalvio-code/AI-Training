# Overview

This source presents perceived alternatives to NexoERP, with all competing
brands being fictional, and defines a factual approach to comparative
advertising.[^fonte-04]

## Main content

- Spreadsheets are a legitimate alternative at the beginning; the problem
  appears when the operation grows and needs a shared process.
- ContaClara, Orbe Gestão, FinPilot, and SaldoSimples serve different profiles,
  with specific capabilities and limitations.
- Internet banking, standalone tools, and financial BPO can also be
  part of the solution or the current process.
- NexoERP stands out by connecting execution, approval, and cash-flow
  visibility, but it should not be presented as the best option for every case.
- Published comparisons must use verifiable capabilities, source, and date,
  without logos used without authorization or absolute claims.

See [Perceived competitors and alternatives](/concepts/concorrentes-e-alternativas.md)
for the comparison matrix and the pricing response.
