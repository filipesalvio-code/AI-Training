# Overview

This source presents NexoERP, a fictional Brazilian cloud financial management
software company, and establishes the foundation for marketing and campaign
creation.[^fonte-01]

## Main content

- The company serves small and medium-sized businesses that have outgrown
  spreadsheets but still do not need a complex corporate ERP.
- The product connects accounts payable and receivable, bank reconciliation,
  cash flow, payment slips, reports, cost centers, and accounting integrations.
- The core problem is rework and low trust caused by data spread across banks,
  spreadsheets, emails, and separate systems.
- The approved promise is **“Financial clarity to decide sooner, not later.”**
- The priority differentiators are guided implementation, a simple cash view,
  integrated reconciliation, audit and approvals, support in Portuguese, and
  modular growth.
- Communication should sell organization, visibility, and operational security,
  with observable benefits, and not just list features.
- The source also defines what NexoERP is not and lists approved phrases and
  claims that should be avoided.
