# Overview

This source defines communication archetypes, not real people, and describes how
each participant relates to purchasing and adopting NexoERP.[^fonte-03]

## Main content

- Fernanda, finance manager, is the main user and leads the evaluation; she
  seeks to reduce rework, close the month with confidence, and create controls.
- Marcelo, owner, monitors cash balance, receivables, and commitments, and often
  approves the budget.
- Caio, financial analyst, executes the routine and can support or block
  adoption depending on the operational experience.
- Helena, external accountant, influences the decision due to the need for
  controlled access, reports, and compatible exports.
- The journey goes from discovery and consideration to decision, adoption, and
  expansion, with different content needs at each stage.

See [Personas and buying journey](/concepts/personas-e-jornada-de-compra.md)
for details on goals, pain points, objections, and useful content.
