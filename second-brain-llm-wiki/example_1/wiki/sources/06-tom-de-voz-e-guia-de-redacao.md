# Overview

This source defines writing that is experienced in finance, respectful of the
reader’s time, explains what is necessary, and offers a next step without using
fear to sell.[^fonte-06]

## Main content

- The tone should be direct, human, precise, and calm.
- Communication uses professional and accessible Brazilian Portuguese, with
  restrictions on slang, emojis, and search ads.
- Ads follow situation, consequence, NexoERP’s help, and call to action.
- CTAs should reflect the journey stage and avoid generic language or
  inappropriate pressure.
- There are rules for titles, acronyms, numbers, currency, AI claims, and
  responses to comments.

See [NexoERP Tone of Voice and Writing Guide](/concepts/tom-de-voz-e-guia-de-redacao.md)
for the full playbook.
