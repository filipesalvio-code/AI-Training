# Overview

This source brings together fictional marketing learnings, experiment results,
and rules for measurement, evidence, privacy, promotion, and incident response at
NexoERP.[^fonte-10]

## Main content

- Specific messaging about forecast vs. actual generated more qualified
  conversations than “total control”; reconciliation ads attract analysts and
  forecasting ads attract owners.
- A readable interface outperformed generic stock imagery in a limited
  consideration sample.
- Headlines that describe tasks and demo CTAs produced better opportunity
  quality, even when some click counts were lower.
- Metrics should follow the stage, from attention to revenue, without optimizing
  only for cheap clicks.
- Numeric claims, testimonials, media lists, fictional data,
  promotions, and incidents require documented controls.
