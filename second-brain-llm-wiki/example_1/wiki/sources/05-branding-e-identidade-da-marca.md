# Overview

This source establishes NexoERP’s identity as a brand that transforms
financial complexity into practical clarity.[^fonte-05]

## Main content

- The purpose is to help companies make more informed decisions about their own
  money.
- The personality is clear, confident, approachable, and precise, without
  oversimplification, authoritarianism, excessive informality, or bureaucracy.
- The main archetype is the guide, with the organizer as support; the customer
  makes the decisions, and the product provides visibility and method.
- The central idea is “Clarity for the next step,” and the institutional
  signature is “NexoERP. Financial clarity to decide in advance.”
- Visibility, connection, operational trust, and evolution are the message
  pillars.
- The name, vocabulary, metaphors, and visual choices have their own rules.

See [NexoERP brand identity](/concepts/identidade-da-marca-nexoerp.md)
for detailed application.
