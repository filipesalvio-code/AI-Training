# Overview

This source defines NexoERP’s ideal customer profile (ICP) to guide
marketing and sales.[^fonte-02] The focus is on the complexity of the financial operation,
not just company revenue.

## Main content

- The primary ICP includes Brazilian companies in services, light distribution,
  technology, private education, or B2B commerce, with annual revenue of R$ 2
  million to R$ 80 million, 10 to 150 employees, a finance team of 1 to 8
  people, two or more bank accounts, and at least 300 monthly entries.
- The best buying moment appears in events such as opening a branch,
  increased billing volume, hiring the first finance leader, or
  dependence on a single person to maintain the spreadsheet.
- B2B services, light distribution, schools and open-enrollment courses, and
  technology companies are the priority segments, each with specific pains and
  messages.
- The secondary ICP may include companies with up to 300 employees when the
  financial structure is still not very complex; these cases require qualification.
- High-intent signals include looking to replace spreadsheets, asking
  about importing data, needing to approve payments, finding discrepancies between
  system and bank, delays in closing, or expanding to a second unit.
- There is low fit with very small operations, individuals, one-off boleto
  needs, complex international consolidation, on-premise installation,
  full outsourcing, or refusal of implementation.
- B2B campaigns should use industry, size, professional context, and
  product-related intent, never sensitive characteristics.

See the [NexoERP ideal customer profile](/concepts/perfil-de-cliente-ideal.md)
for detailed criteria and qualification questions.
