# Overview

This source brings together examples of approved ads for search, LinkedIn,
Instagram, video, remarketing, content, and sponsored email. The copy should
be adapted to the targeting, the offer, and each channel’s limit.[^fonte-08]

## Main content

- Search messages address spreadsheets and reconciliation without promising
  full automation.
- LinkedIn adapts the message for finance managers and owners.
- Instagram and video show signs of growth, connected routines, and cash-flow
  visibility.
- Remarketing and content use a demo, guide, or checklist as the next step,
  without false urgency.
- Unapproved variations include unproven guarantees, aggressive tone, fear,
  unverifiable claims, and fake scarcity.

See [NexoERP approved ads](/concepts/anuncios-aprovados-nexoerp.md)
for channel-organized examples.
