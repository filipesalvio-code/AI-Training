# Overview

This source defines four reference campaigns, the role of each channel, a
launch checklist, and the naming convention used by NexoERP's
marketing.[^fonte-09]

## Main content

- “From spreadsheet to process” seeks qualified opportunities and measures
  meetings, not just leads or downloads.
- “Closing with less rework” focuses on teams that take more than three days
  to reconcile the month.
- “Cash visibility for decision-making” creates demand among owners who look
  only at bank balance.
- “Connected accounting” develops accounting partners without promising to
  replace accounting software or professional obligations.
- Search captures intent, LinkedIn works role and consideration,
  Instagram/video generates awareness, email nurtures with legal basis, and
  partners need transparency.
- Launch requires review of audience, offer, conversion, claims,
  consents, parameters, support, date, and owner.
