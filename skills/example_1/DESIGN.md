# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World

Atmospheric radar station: a high-contrast observation surface where the city result appears as a live meteorological field rather than a generic forecast card.

## Surface: weather search

- **Mode:** Operate
- **Primary task:** Search for a city and read its current conditions immediately.
- **Composition:** Identity and one decisive heading lead into the search control. The returned reading resolves underneath as one focused instrument panel: place, condition, temperature, then metrics.
- **Color strategy:** Drenched, data-driven palette. Each weather condition selects the complete field: amber/red for hot clear skies; electric blue for cool clear skies and rain; pale ice for snow; violet for storms; blue-violet for night.
- **Materials:** Luminous radar rings, atmospheric orbs, translucent measurement panel, crisp line icons.
- **Type:** Unbounded for measured display moments; Manrope for controls and reading text.
- **Motion:** The atmosphere drifts gently and a resolved result enters once from a soft blur. Reduced-motion users receive a still surface.
- **Responsive:** Search stacks only at narrow width; the primary temperature stays dominant and metrics preserve three equal columns.

## Accessibility

Text and interactive controls retain high contrast across every condition palette. Search feedback is announced in a polite live region; errors use an alert; focus remains clearly visible; decorative scene elements are hidden from assistive technology.
